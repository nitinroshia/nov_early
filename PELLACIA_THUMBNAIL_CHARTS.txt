# PELLACIA — FINANCIAL METRIC THUMBNAIL CHART SPECIFICATION

Chart size: 80 × 31 px
Annual history: 5 fiscal years
Quarterly design: 9 horizontal bars (3 groups × 3 bars)
Annual design: 3 separate grouped bars (3 metrics × 5 fiscal years)

---

# 1\. INCOME STATEMENT (IS)

## QUARTERLY — 9 HORIZONTAL BARS

Blue — Revenue

- Total Revenue
- Operating Revenue
- Other Revenue

Red — Expenses

- Total Expenses
- Operating Expenses
- Non-operating Expenses

Green — Profitability

- Net Income
- Operating Income
- Pre-tax Income

Note: These are separate reported metrics, not additive components. Tax expense is reflected in the calculation of net income.

## ANNUAL — 3 GROUPED BARS

- Blue: Revenue
- Red: Expenses
- Green: Net Income

---

# 2\. BALANCE SHEET (BS)

## QUARTERLY — 9 HORIZONTAL BARS

Blue — Assets

- Total Assets
- Current Assets
- Non-current Assets

Red — Liabilities

- Total Liabilities
- Current Liabilities
- Non-current Liabilities

Green — Shareholders' Equity

- Total Equity
- Share Capital
- Retained Earnings / Reserves

Note: Share capital and retained earnings do not necessarily represent all components of total equity.

## ANNUAL — 3 GROUPED BARS

- Blue: Total Assets
- Red: Total Liabilities
- Green: Total Equity

---

# 3\. CASH FLOW STATEMENT (CF)

## QUARTERLY — 9 HORIZONTAL BARS

Green — Operating Activities

- Operating Cash Flow
- Net Income
- Change in Working Capital

Red — Investing Activities

- Investing Cash Flow
- Capital Expenditure
- Acquisitions / Disposals

Blue — Financing Activities

- Financing Cash Flow
- Debt Issuance / Repayment
- Share Issuance / Repurchases

## ANNUAL — 3 GROUPED BARS

- Green: Operating Cash Flow
- Red: Investing Cash Flow
- Blue: Financing Cash Flow

Note: Cash flow values retain their positive or negative direction. The quarterly supporting metrics are not necessarily additive to the headline cash flow without the other reconciliation items.

---

# 4\. PROFITABILITY AND RETURNS (PR)

## QUARTERLY — 9 HORIZONTAL BARS

Blue — Gross Profitability

- Gross Profit Margin
- Cost of Goods Sold %
- Gross Profit Growth

Green — Operating Profitability

- Operating Profit Margin
- EBITDA Margin
- Operating Income Growth

Amber — Returns

- Return on Equity (ROE)
- Return on Invested Capital (ROIC)
- Return on Assets (ROA)

## ANNUAL — 3 GROUPED BARS

- Blue: Gross Profit Margin
- Green: Operating Profit Margin
- Amber: Return on Equity (ROE)

Note: ROIC and ROA can be available as supporting historical metrics. Do not combine independent return ratios into one additive value.

---

# 5\. GROWTH AND HISTORICAL PERFORMANCE (GH)

## QUARTERLY — 9 HORIZONTAL BARS

Blue — Revenue Growth

- Revenue YoY Growth
- Revenue QoQ Growth
- Revenue Growth Rate

Green — Earnings Growth

- Net Income YoY Growth
- EPS YoY Growth
- EBITDA YoY Growth

Amber — Cash Flow Growth

- Operating Cash Flow YoY Growth
- Free Cash Flow YoY Growth
- Cash Flow Growth Rate

## ANNUAL — 3 GROUPED BARS

- Blue: Revenue Growth
- Green: Earnings Growth
- Amber: Free Cash Flow Growth

Note: Annual growth represents year-over-year growth across five fiscal years. CAGR can be provided as an additional metric.

---

# 6\. FINANCIAL HEALTH AND EFFICIENCY (FH)

## QUARTERLY — 9 HORIZONTAL BARS

Blue — Liquidity

- Current Ratio
- Quick Ratio
- Cash Ratio

Green — Working Capital Efficiency

- Cash Conversion Cycle
- Inventory Days
- Receivable Days

Amber — Asset Efficiency

- Asset Turnover
- Inventory Turnover
- Receivables Turnover

## ANNUAL — 3 GROUPED BARS

- Blue: Current Ratio
- Green: Cash Conversion Cycle
- Amber: Asset Turnover

Note: These metrics use different units. Compare each metric's historical trend independently, not by raw bar length across categories.

---

# 7\. VALUATION (VL)

## QUARTERLY — 9 HORIZONTAL BARS

Blue — Earnings Valuation

- P/E Ratio
- Forward P/E
- EV/EBITDA

Green — Sales Valuation

- P/S Ratio
- EV/Sales
- Price / Gross Profit

Amber — Cash Flow Valuation

- P/FCF
- P/OCF
- EV/FCF

## ANNUAL — 3 GROUPED BARS

- Blue: Earnings Valuation (P/E)
- Green: Sales Valuation (EV/Sales)
- Amber: Cash Flow Valuation (P/FCF)

Note: Use consistent fiscal-year-end valuation dates. Exclude or flag multiples that are not meaningful because of negative or near-zero denominators.

---

# 8\. SHAREHOLDING AND OWNERSHIP (SO)

## QUARTERLY — 9 HORIZONTAL BARS

Blue — Promoter Ownership

- Promoter Holding %
- Promoter Pledge %
- Promoter Holding Change

Green — Institutional Ownership

- Institutional Holding %
- FII Holding %
- DII Holding %

Amber — Public Ownership

- Public Holding %
- Retail Holding %
- Other Shareholders %

## ANNUAL — 3 GROUPED BARS

- Blue: Promoter Ownership
- Green: Institutional Ownership
- Amber: Public Ownership

Note: FII and DII are components of institutional ownership. Retail is generally a component of public ownership. Do not add overlapping ownership categories. Promoter pledge is measured as a percentage of promoter holdings.

---

# 9\. MARKET ACTIVITY AND PRICE PERFORMANCE (MP)

## QUARTERLY — 9 HORIZONTAL BARS

Blue — Price Performance

- Price Change %
- Distance from 52-Week High
- Distance from 52-Week Low

Green — Trading Activity

- Average Daily Volume
- Volume Change %
- Delivery Volume %

Amber — Market Risk

- Historical Volatility
- Beta
- Maximum Drawdown

## ANNUAL — 3 GROUPED BARS

- Blue: Annual Price Return %
- Green: Trading Volume Growth %
- Amber: Annualized Volatility %

Note: Use comparable trading periods and consistent calculation windows. Price returns should account for corporate actions and dividends where appropriate.

---

# 10\. RISK AND RISK-ADJUSTED PERFORMANCE (RK)

## QUARTERLY — 9 HORIZONTAL BARS

Blue — Market Risk

- Beta
- Historical Volatility
- Maximum Drawdown

Green — Risk-adjusted Returns

- Sharpe Ratio
- Sortino Ratio
- Treynor Ratio

Amber — Financial Risk

- Debt-to-Equity
- Interest Coverage
- Net Debt / EBITDA

## ANNUAL — 3 GROUPED BARS

- Blue: Market Risk (Beta)
- Green: Risk-adjusted Returns (Sharpe Ratio)
- Amber: Financial Risk (Debt-to-Equity)

Note: Risk and return metrics must use consistent observation periods and calculation methodologies.

---

# GLOBAL CHART DESIGN RULES

1. All thumbnail charts must be exactly 80 × 31 px.
2. Quarterly charts must contain 9 horizontal bars.
3. Quarterly bars must be organized into 3 groups of 3.
4. Annual charts must contain 3 separate grouped bars.
5. Annual charts must display 5 fiscal years.
6. Maintain consistent category colors across all 10 metrics.
7. Blue, green and amber represent the three primary groups, with red reserved for expenses and investing cash flow where specified.
8. Do not stack independent financial ratios, percentages or unrelated metrics.
9. Stack values only when they represent genuinely additive components.
10. Negative values must retain their direction around an appropriate zero baseline.
11. Metrics with different units must not be compared using misleading shared numerical scales.
12. Quarterly and annual thumbnails must use consistent metric definitions and calculation methodologies.

END OF SPECIFICATION
